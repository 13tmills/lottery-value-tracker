What NumbersIntel covers, state by state

NumbersIntel tracks the draw games of 47 US jurisdictions — every state that runs a lottery, plus Washington, D.C. and Puerto Rico — roughly 150 games in all. That ranges from multi-million-dollar jackpot games like New York Lotto, Lotto Texas and California SuperLotto Plus down to the twice-daily Pick 3 and Pick 4 digit games nearly every state runs. Each state page gathers that state's games in one place, with the latest winning numbers, recent results, the full odds and prize structure, and — for games whose prizes allow it — the real expected value of a ticket.

For every game we compute the odds directly from its number matrix and, where the prize table is fixed, the value a $1 ticket returns after taxes. We also track number-frequency history — the most and least common numbers, hot and cold trends and overdue numbers — with the honest caveat that draws are random: these statistics describe the past and do not predict the next drawing. No number is ever "due."

The national games are everywhere

Wherever you live, the three multi-state games — Powerball, Mega Millions and Lotto America — are drawn the same way and carry the same odds. See them side by side, ranked by expected value, on the US National Drawings page.
