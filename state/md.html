The most winnable game in Maryland

Of the 5 Maryland draw games we track, Bonus Match 5 has the shortest odds on its top prize at 1 in 575,757 ($1 a play). Shorter odds mean a smaller prize — that is the trade every lottery makes — but it is the game in Maryland where the top prize is least out of reach. Compare every state.

Which numbers come up most in Bonus Match 5?

Across 1,042 Bonus Match 5 draws on its current 1–39 number pool (since 2023), the most-drawn numbers are 4 (151), 10 (148), 22 (147), 38 (147), 5 (144). The least-drawn are 21 (119), 34 (118), 8 (116), 7 (114), 39 (113).

The commonest number leads the rarest by about 34% — and that means nothing. Across this many draws ordinary random variation produces spreads of roughly this size; a fair game would almost never land every number on an identical count. No number is "due", and none of this predicts the next draw. We only count draws from the game's current number pool, because earlier draws used a different one and mixing them invents a pattern that was never there.
