The 4 Connecticut draw games we track — odds, prices and what each is actually worth.

Most lottery sites stop at the winning numbers. For each of Connecticut's 4 draw games we publish the exact odds from the game's own number matrix, the full prize table, and where the prizes are fixed, the expected value per dollar — the figure that says what a ticket is really worth.

The most winnable game in Connecticut

Of the 4 Connecticut draw games we track, Cash5 has the shortest odds on its top prize at 1 in 324,632 ($1 a play). Shorter odds mean a smaller prize — that is the trade every lottery makes — but it is the game in Connecticut where the top prize is least out of reach. Compare every state.

Which numbers come up most in Play3?

Across 10,279 Play3 draws on its current 0–9 number pool (since 1998), the most-drawn numbers are 1 (3189), 0 (3177), 9 (3131), 5 (3112), 7 (3084). The least-drawn are 2 (3070), 4 (3064), 3 (3047), 6 (3006), 8 (2957).

The commonest number leads the rarest by about 8% — and that means nothing. Across this many draws ordinary random variation produces spreads of roughly this size; a fair game would almost never land every number on an identical count. No number is "due", and none of this predicts the next draw. We only count draws from the game's current number pool, because earlier draws used a different one and mixing them invents a pattern that was never there.
