Most lottery sites stop at the winning numbers. For each of Florida's 9 draw games we publish the exact odds from the game's own number matrix, the full prize table, and where the prizes are fixed, the expected value per dollar — the figure that says what a ticket is really worth.

The most winnable game in Florida

Of the 9 Florida draw games we track, Fantasy 5 has the shortest odds on its top prize at 1 in 376,992 ($1 a play). Shorter odds mean a smaller prize — that is the trade every lottery makes — but it is the game in Florida where the top prize is least out of reach. Compare every state.
