The most winnable game in Virginia

Of the 5 Virginia draw games we track, Cash 5 has the shortest odds on its top prize at 1 in 1,221,759 ($1 a play). Shorter odds mean a smaller prize — that is the trade every lottery makes — but it is the game in Virginia where the top prize is least out of reach. Compare every state.

Which numbers come up most in Pick 3?

Across 12,667 Pick 3 draws on its current 0–9 number pool (since 1989), the most-drawn numbers are 8 (3861), 6 (3830), 2 (3827), 1 (3813), 5 (3813). The least-drawn are 7 (3804), 3 (3790), 9 (3778), 0 (3764), 4 (3721).

The commonest number leads the rarest by about 4% — and that means nothing. Across this many draws ordinary random variation produces spreads of roughly this size; a fair game would almost never land every number on an identical count. No number is "due", and none of this predicts the next draw. We only count draws from the game's current number pool, because earlier draws used a different one and mixing them invents a pattern that was never there.
