How Pick 5 works

Pick 5 is a five-digit game: instead of chasing a jackpot, you choose a 5-digit number (from 00000 to 99999) and win a fixed amount set by how you bet. A Straight matches all 5 digits in exact order and pays the top prize of $50,000 — a 1 in 100,000 shot — while a Box matches the same digits in any order for a smaller prize that comes up more often. Maryland draws it Twice daily at $0.50 or $1 a play.

Digit games are the most transparent corner of the lottery: the odds are short, every payout is fixed and published up front, and there is no rolling jackpot to build. That also makes the house edge easy to see — the lottery keeps a set share of every dollar wagered no matter which number you pick, so it stays a negative-expected-value bet played for fun, not profit.

Our archive holds 227 Pick 5 draws going back to 2025, which powers the number-frequency statistics — the most and least common numbers, current hot and cold streaks, and the longest-overdue numbers. Those describe what has already happened; because each draw is independent, no number is ever truly ‘due.’
