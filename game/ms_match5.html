How Mississippi Match 5 works

Mississippi Match 5 is a Mississippi draw game built around a top prize you win by matching all of the numbers drawn — about 1 in 324,632. That prize typically starts at a set minimum and rolls higher every drawing it goes unclaimed, which is how these games can build for weeks before someone finally hits them. It is drawn Daily at $2 a play.

The lower prize tiers are far easier to hit and pay smaller, mostly fixed amounts, so most winning tickets never touch the top prize. NumbersIntel derives the odds for every tier from the game's published rules. Like every lottery game it is negative-expected-value by design; the higher the top prize climbs, the more a ticket is worth — but also the likelier that prize is to be split between multiple winners.

Our archive holds 1,368 Mississippi Match 5 draws going back to 2021, which powers the number-frequency statistics — the most and least common numbers, current hot and cold streaks, and the longest-overdue numbers. Those describe what has already happened; because each draw is independent, no number is ever truly ‘due.’

Most and least common Mississippi Match 5 numbers

Across 1,368 draws on the current 1–35 number pool (since 2021), the numbers drawn most often are 1 (225), 33 (218), 13 (212), 28 (212), 21 (211), 22 (210). The least often: 15 (183), 30 (183), 32 (176), 11 (174), 12 (172), 14 (171).

The commonest leads the rarest by about 32%, and that means nothing at all. Over this many draws ordinary random variation produces spreads of roughly this size — a fair game would almost never land every number on an identical count. No number is "due", and none of this predicts the next draw. We count only draws from the game's current number pool, because earlier draws used a different one and mixing them manufactures a pattern that was never there.
