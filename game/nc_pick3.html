How Pick 3 works

Pick 3 is a three-digit game: instead of chasing a jackpot, you choose a 3-digit number (from 000 to 999) and win a fixed amount set by how you bet. A Straight matches all 3 digits in exact order and pays the top prize of $500 — a 1 in 1,000 shot — while a Box matches the same digits in any order for a smaller prize that comes up more often. North Carolina draws it Twice daily at $0.50 or $1 a play.

Digit games are the most transparent corner of the lottery: the odds are short, every payout is fixed and published up front, and there is no rolling jackpot to build. That also makes the house edge easy to see — the lottery keeps a set share of every dollar wagered no matter which number you pick, so it stays a negative-expected-value bet played for fun, not profit.

Our archive holds 7,261 Pick 3 draws going back to 2006, which powers the number-frequency statistics — the most and least common numbers, current hot and cold streaks, and the longest-overdue numbers. Those describe what has already happened; because each draw is independent, no number is ever truly ‘due.’
