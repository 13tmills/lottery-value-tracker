How Bonus Match 5 works

Bonus Match 5 is a Maryland lottery game drawn Nightly, with tickets from $1. NumbersIntel gathers its latest results, full odds and prize structure, and number-frequency history in one place.

We compute the odds directly from the game's published rules, so you can see exactly what each prize tier is worth. As with every lottery game, the expected value of a ticket is below what you pay for it — it is entertainment, not an investment.

Our archive holds 1,025 Bonus Match 5 draws going back to 2023, which powers the number-frequency statistics — the most and least common numbers, current hot and cold streaks, and the longest-overdue numbers. Those describe what has already happened; because each draw is independent, no number is ever truly ‘due.’
