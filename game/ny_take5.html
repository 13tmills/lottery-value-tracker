How Take 5 works

Take 5 is a New York lottery game drawn Daily (evening), with tickets from $1. NumbersIntel gathers its latest results, full odds and prize structure, and number-frequency history in one place.

We compute the odds directly from the game's published rules, so you can see exactly what each prize tier is worth. As with every lottery game, the expected value of a ticket is below what you pay for it — it is entertainment, not an investment.

Our archive holds 10,712 Take 5 draws going back to 1992, which powers the number-frequency statistics — the most and least common numbers, current hot and cold streaks, and the longest-overdue numbers. Those describe what has already happened; because each draw is independent, no number is ever truly ‘due.’
