Powerball

Odds, prizes, the latest winning numbers and recent results.

Powerball is a draw game, held every . As of the latest update its advertised jackpot is $416M (a cash value of about $186.6M), and the odds of matching all six numbers are 1 in 292,201,338. Run the real expected-value math and a $2 ticket is worth about 30.2¢ per dollar at this jackpot — meaning roughly 70¢ of every dollar is the house edge. Among the three national games, that is currently the second-best value. Like every lottery game it is a negative-expected-value bet by design; these figures show which is least bad, and by how much.

About this data

Every figure here comes from official lottery sources, refreshed after each drawing. We compute the odds from the game's published rules and, where the prize structure allows, the expected value of a ticket. See our methodology page for the full method, and the guides on expected value, how the odds work and whether hot numbers win.
