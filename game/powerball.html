Powerball

Expected value, exact odds, the full prize table and the latest winning numbers.

Powerball is a 5 of 69 white balls plus 1 of 26 red Power Balls draw game, held every Monday, Wednesday and Saturday. As of the latest update its advertised jackpot is $396M (a cash value of about $180.2M), and the odds of matching all six numbers are 1 in 292,201,338. Run the real expected-value math and a $2 ticket is worth about 29.5¢ per dollar at this jackpot — meaning roughly 70¢ of every dollar is the house edge. Among the three national games, that is currently the second-best value. Like every lottery game it is a negative-expected-value bet by design; these figures simply show which is least bad, and by how much.

Will the Powerball jackpot be won or split?

Using the number of lower-tier winners each recent draw produced, we estimate about ~17.5 million tickets are in play at the current jackpot — roughly a 5.8% chance that someone wins the jackpot, and a 3.0% chance it would be split between multiple winners if it is hit. How we compute Powerball's value

Every figure here comes from official sources and the game's published prize structure, refreshed after each drawing. The odds are computed from the number matrix (5 of 69 white balls plus 1 of 26 red Power Balls); the expected value sums each prize tier against its probability after an assumed tax rate. We document the full method on our methodology page. For the ideas behind these numbers, see what expected value means, how the odds work, and lump sum vs annuity if you are weighing how to take a jackpot.
